A quick word on storing your water.

  • Store packs upright in a cool, dry space — a pantry, store room, or kitchen corner works well.
  • Avoid direct sunlight and keep away from strong-smelling chemicals or cleaning products.
  • Sealed packs stay fresh. Once a pack is opened, finish it within the week.
