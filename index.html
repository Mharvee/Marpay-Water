About

What is Marpay Plus?

Marpay Plus is a subscription-based water delivery service from Marpay Water. Subscribe once and receive quality 50cl bottled water delivered to your home, office, event venue, or store — on a schedule that works for you.

At ₦1,500 per pack, you get near-factory pricing without the factory run. Just subscribe, tell us your location, and your water comes to you.
